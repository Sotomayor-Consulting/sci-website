Currently, thousands of Latin American entrepreneurs and business owners are using LLCs in the United States to sell international services, operate e-commerce, earn in dollars, and access the U.S. financial system.

However, there is also a lot of misinformation.

Many people believe that:

- An LLC automatically eliminates taxes.
- It is only for large companies.
- You need to live in the U.S.
- It takes months to open.
- There are no obligations after incorporating.

The reality is very different.

In this complete guide, we will explain what an LLC is, how it works, and what it is really for.

## What does LLC mean?

LLC stands for **Limited Liability Company**.

It is a U.S. business structure that combines:

- Asset protection.
- Operational flexibility.
- Adaptable tax options.

The LLC is one of the most used structures by:

- Entrepreneurs.
- Digital businesses.
- Consultants.
- E-commerce.
- Investors.
- International companies.

## How does an LLC work in the United States?

An LLC functions as a legal entity separate from its owners.

This means the company can:

- Have bank accounts.
- Sign contracts.
- Invoice clients.
- Receive payments.
- Hold assets.
- Operate commercially.

Depending on how it is structured, the LLC may have different tax treatments before the IRS.

## What is an LLC for?

An LLC can be used for multiple business and commercial objectives.

### Asset protection

One of the main benefits is helping to separate:

- Personal assets.
- Business assets.

This can reduce risks against business obligations, depending on proper corporate management.

### Earn in dollars

Many entrepreneurs use LLCs to:

- Receive international payments.
- Operate in dollars.
- Access financial platforms.

### Access to banks and fintechs

An LLC can facilitate access to:

- Business bank accounts.
- Fintechs.
- Payment processors.
- International platforms.

### Operate internationally

LLCs are widely used by:

- Service exporters.
- E-commerce.
- Digital agencies.
- SaaS.
- Freelancers.
- International consultants.

## Main benefits of an LLC

### Business professionalization

- Clients.

## Who can open an LLC?

One of the biggest advantages is that **you do not need to be a U.S. citizen**.

A non-resident alien can open an LLC in the United States.

In many cases you also do not need:

- Green Card.
- SSN.
- U.S. residency.

## Does an LLC protect personal assets?

The LLC helps legally separate the person from the company.

However, this protection depends on:

- Proper corporate management.
- Financial separation.
- Legal and tax compliance.

For example, mixing personal and business expenses can create significant risks.

## Does an LLC pay taxes in the U.S.?

**It depends.** The taxation of an LLC depends on factors such as:

- Owner's tax residency.
- Type of activity.
- Source of income.
- Physical presence in the U.S.
- Chosen structure.

Some LLCs have informational, tax, and state obligations.

That is why each case must be analyzed individually.

## Difference between LLC and Corporation

- **Flexibility:** More flexible -- More structured
- **Formalities:** Fewer formalities -- More corporate formalities
- **Use by foreigners:** Widely used -- Less common
- **Shares:** Does not use shares -- Uses shares
- **Ideal for:** Digital businesses, freelancers -- Startups with investors
The best option will depend on the business model.

## What is the best state to open an LLC?

The most common states are:

- **Wyoming:** Low costs, privacy, simplicity
- **Florida:** Latin American entrepreneurs, real estate
- **Delaware:** Startups, holdings, companies with investors
There is no "universal best state."

## Do I need to travel to the U.S. to open an LLC?

In many cases: **No**. Much of the process can be done remotely.

However, some banks may require physical presence depending on the case.

## What documents do I need to incorporate an LLC?

Normally required:

- Valid passport.
- Personal address.
- Company name.
- Business activity information.
- Email.
- Phone number.

After incorporating, you typically obtain:

- Certificate of Formation.
- Operating Agreement.
- EIN.

## Common mistakes when opening an LLC

1. Thinking "LLC = zero taxes."
2. Choosing the wrong state.
3. Not filing forms with the IRS.
4. Mixing personal and business expenses.
5. Opening bank accounts unprepared.
6. Copying internet structures without advice.

## Frequently Asked Questions about LLCs

### Does an LLC grant residency in the United States?
No. An LLC is a business structure, not an immigration one.

### Can a foreigner open an LLC?
Yes.

### Do I need to travel to the U.S.?
Not necessarily.

### What is an EIN?
It is the company's tax identification number before the IRS.

### Does an LLC eliminate taxes?
Not automatically. Everything depends on the structure and activity.

### What bank can I use with an LLC?
It depends on the business profile and banking compliance.

### What happens if I never filed taxes?
There could be fines or pending obligations depending on the case.
